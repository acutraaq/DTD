# Jalinan Merdeka — "The Trace" Storyboard Prompt — Design

**Date:** 2026-08-11
**Deliverable:** One rebuilt GPT Image 2 master storyboard prompt (18 frames, 6×3 contact sheet) for the Jalinan Merdeka film.
**Audience:** DTD staff, townhall screening. Insider recognition ("that's us / that's our division") is a deliberate payoff.

## Design decisions (approved in brainstorm)

1. **Structure — "The Trace" (reverse mystery).** The v1 draft showed 1957 first and captioned the connection away by frame 3, killing the reveal. Rebuilt structure opens in 2026 with an anomalous signal of unknown origin; the film is a trace run backwards through the network and through time, terminating at 31.08.1957 00:00. Merdeka becomes the *answer to the mystery*, not a patriotic prologue. Double reveal: (1) the signal IS the network (Jalinan), (2) its origin is independence itself.
2. **Captions = system trace-log.** No narrator voice. Captions are cold telemetry that itself has an arc: technical → confused → parse failure → first human sentence at the reveal → silence at 1957 → final thesis line. Exactly two question marks in the whole film (`PERSONNEL?`, `BACKWARDS?`). Frames 10 and 15 (image) carry deliberate log silence.
3. **No flag before frame 15.** Origin stays secret. Frame 15 inherits first-warm-colour privilege because it is the answer.
4. **Anyaman sharpened.** Kelarai mat-weaving geometry (diagonal over-under interlace, visible weave rhythm) rendered in light strands. Explicit negatives: no generic node-edge graph, no plexus effect.
5. **The Seven.** DAID, DCSD, DDD, DESD, DID, DSD, DSMD each get one distinguishing physical environment cue so staff recognize their own room.
6. **Cinematography system (user-supplied, verbatim in prompt).** Practical-scale large-format digital, anamorphic 2.39:1, single motivated key camera-left, cool steel-blue shadow fill. Accent rule: warm amber default, **cyan instead in thread-active shots**. Since frames 1–14 are thread-active and 15–18 progressively are not, this rule mechanically produces the film's colour journey (cyan world → amber/daylight world). Imperfection layer and both negative blocks included verbatim.
7. **Format.** 18 frames, 6 columns × 3 rows, thin black borders, bold black frame numbers top-left, caption bar under each frame.

## Out of scope

- Video generation prompts / shot-by-shot animation prompts (later step, after board approval).
- Changes to the film treatment doc (`docs/jalinan-merdeka-signal-treatment.md`) — the Trace restructure supersedes its beat order for the storyboard; treatment update deferred until board is approved by stakeholders.
- Multiple sheets / per-beat hero images.

# THE PROMPT (ready to paste into GPT Image 2)

Create a premium cinematic storyboard contact sheet for a 3–4 minute Malaysian cinematic technology thriller titled **JALINAN MERDEKA — THE SIGNAL**.

This is NOT a corporate video. It is structured as a mystery: an unknown signal is discovered inside a national digital infrastructure, traced backwards through the network — and backwards through time — until its origin is revealed to be the midnight of Malaysian independence, 31 August 1957. The audience must not understand the signal until the film reveals it. The story unfolds visually; nothing is explained.

## FORMAT

Exactly **18 frames in a 6 × 3 grid**. Every frame must contain: a thin black border; a bold black sans-serif frame number in the top-left; a short caption bar beneath the frame; cinematic 2.39:1 widescreen composition; consistent visual continuity, character design and environments; chronological storytelling. Professional director's storyboard / visual-development board. Not a comic book. No speech bubbles. No stock-photo look.

## CAPTION SYSTEM — THE TRACE LOG

Captions are NOT narration. They are cold system-telemetry lines from a network trace, uppercase, terse. The log itself evolves: purely technical at first, then confused, then failing to parse, then — at the reveal — its first human sentence, then silence at the 1957 origin, then one final line. Use each frame's caption exactly as specified below.

## CINEMATOGRAPHY (applies to every frame)

Practical-scale digital cinematography, large-format sensor feel, visible fine natural film grain, no artificial digital sheen, no plastic/glossy CGI quality. Anamorphic 2.39:1 character throughout, oval bokeh, horizontal flare only when motivated by a practical light source. Hard motivated key light from camera-left as the single dominant source, deep shadow falloff into true shadow, no soft shadowless ambient fill. Cool steel-blue ambient fill dominant in shadow zones; the motivated accent light is warm amber by default (window, lamp, golden-hour) — EXCEPT in shots carrying an active signal thread, where the motivated accent is strong saturated cyan instead. Desaturated grade — cool blue-grey base throughout, restrained accent colour (amber, or cyan in thread shots) only where the motivated practical source lands, never a full colour wash. Deliberate weighted camera moves only, no floaty drift, no whip-pans; static/locked-off holds on key resolve frames. Physical camera weight, subtle grounded operator character where handheld. Photoreal naturalistic skin tones and fabric texture where people are in frame, contrast-heavy but never crushing facial detail. In thread shots: near-monochrome deep navy-black base with cyan as the sole motivated light source, thread/node system always legible, never a dense tangle.

Slightly imperfect exposure across the frame rather than uniformly balanced — natural falloff, minor hot spots and underexposed corners where a real lens and real light would produce them. Sensor noise visible in shadow detail, not just decorative grain on top of a clean image. Focus not razor-sharp edge-to-edge — natural focus fall-off, occasional soft edges consistent with a real depth of field rather than an artificially uniform plane. Slight asymmetry in framing and composition — not perfectly centred, not perfectly balanced, as a human operator would actually frame it. Minor lens imperfections where motivated: slight vignetting, faint chromatic fringing only at extreme high-contrast edges (not a stylised aberration effect), subtle barrel or pincushion distortion consistent with real anamorphic glass. Skin and fabric carry visible texture, pores, minor asymmetries, flyaway hairs, creases — nothing airbrushed or uniformly smooth. Environments show wear: scuffs, dust, uneven surfaces, minor clutter, imperfect tidiness — nothing showroom-clean. Camera operator imperfection where handheld: micro-drift, small correcting adjustments, weight and inertia in the movement rather than a mathematically smooth glide.

## THE SIGNAL MOTIF

One recurring **single thin cyan thread of light**. It is NOT a laser. It is NOT a hologram. It reads as light + data + memory + connection. In frames 1–14 it is the active accent light source. From frame 15 onward it fades progressively; by frame 18 it is almost invisible and motivated warm amber daylight has taken over.

## COLOUR JOURNEY (hard per-act palette locks)

- Frames 1–11: near-monochrome — black, graphite, dark steel, cool blue-grey; cyan is the ONLY accent. Zero warm pixels. No flag anywhere.
- Frames 12–14: cyan becomes luminous and elegant, never chaotic.
- Frame 15: FIRST warm colour of the film — controlled tungsten/firelight warmth on the 1957 scene.
- Frames 16–18: warmth grows, cyan decays to a faint echo; frame 18 is natural warm dawn daylight.

### FRAME 01 — ANOMALY
Dead of night inside a vast DTD server hall. Monumental dark corridor of racks, graphite and steel, huge negative space. Every status light beats in rhythm — except one rack, where a tiny cyan pulse blinks out of sync. Almost imperceptible. Locked-off composition.
Caption: **"ANOMALY DETECTED — 03:12"**

### FRAME 02 — WRONG DIRECTION
Extreme macro on circuitry and fibre. The thin cyan thread moves through the infrastructure — visibly *against* the flow of all other data traffic. Shallow depth of field, oval bokeh, sensor noise in the shadows.
Caption: **"SIGNAL PROFILE: UNKNOWN. NOT INBOUND. NOT OUTBOUND."**

### FRAME 03 — THE OPS ROOM
Network operations room at night. Malaysian engineers lit only by monitor glow and one hard key from camera-left. Faces concerned, leaning in. No dialogue, no readable screen text. Human, real, worn environment — coffee cups, cable clutter.
Caption: **"NO SOURCE FOUND"**

### FRAME 04 — IT LEAVES THE BUILDING
Exterior night. The government building dark against the sky; a hairline of cyan slips out of the architecture and into the city grid below. The audience realises the signal is not contained.
Caption: **"TRACE INITIATED"**

### FRAME 05 — HOP 1: THE CITY
Massive cinematic aerial of Kuala Lumpur at night — thousands of sodium and white city lights, desaturated. One continuous thin cyan vein threads through the city fabric. Scale suddenly expands.
Caption: **"HOP 1 — LOCATION: KUALA LUMPUR"**

### FRAME 06 — HOP 2: PEOPLE
Rapid montage grid within the frame: close-ups of eyes, hands on keyboards, a reflection in spectacles, a hand pulling fibre, a face lit by a screen. Ordinary unnamed DTD staff, different rooms. The cyan thread passes between and *through* the human moments.
Caption: **"HOP 2 — SIGNAL PASSING THROUGH... PERSONNEL?"**

### FRAME 07 — HOP 3: THE SEVEN
Seven distinct DTD division environments composed in one frame — DAID, DCSD, DDD, DESD, DID, DSD, DSMD — each visually different (an ops video wall; a data-centre cold aisle; a development floor with dual monitors; a records/archive room; an infrastructure plant room; a service counter; a strategy meeting room with printed plans). Each contains the same faint cyan pulse. Matched compositions begin to rhyme.
Caption: **"HOP 3 — SIGNAL PRESENT IN ALL SEVEN DIVISIONS. SIMULTANEOUSLY."**

### FRAME 08 — THE INDEX ERROR
Darker, tighter frame. A faint diegetic trace-readout hangs in the gloom of a server aisle: a timestamp counter visibly running BACKWARDS — 2026… 2011… 1998. The cyan thread accelerates past camera. Unease.
Caption: **"TEMPORAL INDEX ERROR — TRACE RUNNING... BACKWARDS?"**

### FRAME 09 — THE CHASE
The most kinetic frame of the board. The camera hurtles after the cyan thread: server aisle, corridor, office, street, aerial — layered in one aggressive motion-blurred composition, hard perspective lines, controlled operator violence. Faint year fragments fall away in the blur: 1981… 1969…
Caption: **"HOP COUNT LOST — ACCELERATING"**

### FRAME 10 — THE STOP
Everything frozen. People mid-stride, status lights held, the thread suspended mid-air. Absolute stillness, enormous negative space, near-black frame. Locked-off. No motion anywhere.
Caption: **"TRACE HALTED"**

### FRAME 11 — CANNOT PARSE
Almost pure black frame. One thin horizontal cyan line, dead centre-left, breathing faintly. Nothing else.
Caption: **"DATA FORMAT UNKNOWN. NOT MACHINE DATA. PARSING... PARSING..."**

### FRAME 12 — THE REVEAL
One enormous pull-back composition: a single person at a desk, nested inside an office, inside a division floor, inside the building, inside the city, inside the outline of Malaysia — all visible in one continuous receding perspective, connected by ONE unbroken cyan thread. What looked like separate places was always one structure.
Caption: **"IT WAS NEVER SEPARATE."**

### FRAME 13 — JALINAN
Thousands of cyan threads interweave into a monumental structure built on the geometry of Malay kelarai mat-weaving: diagonal over-under interlacing strands, visible weave rhythm, continuity and collective strength — fused with the language of data infrastructure. Sophisticated, monumental, elegant. NOT a node-edge network graph, NOT a plexus effect, NOT decorative folklore.
Caption: **"PATTERN RECOGNIZED: JALINAN"**

### FRAME 14 — INTELLIGENCE
The woven structure is reorganising itself: strands repositioning ahead of need, new over-under connections forming before they are required, the pattern anticipating. Emergent intelligence expressed purely as behaviour of the weave — no robot, no brain, no interface. Precise, almost alive.
Caption: **"SYSTEM STATE: ANTICIPATING"**

### FRAME 15 — ORIGIN
Split emphasis: at the top of the frame, on black, the trace's terminal line — "ORIGIN LOCATED — 31.08.1957 — 00:00". Below it, the image the trace found: night, 1957, a crowd in silhouette, atmospheric haze, the Malaysian flag rising on its pole — and deep in the darkness, the very first tiny cyan flicker. FIRST warm colour of the entire film: controlled tungsten/firelight warmth on fabric and faces.
Caption: *(no log line — silence)*

### FRAME 16 — 69 YEARS
Time runs forward through one flowing composition: 1957 → Stadium Merdeka → decades of national development → modern infrastructure → Merdeka 118 at dusk. The cyan thread runs continuously through every era, growing fainter as warmth grows stronger.
Caption: **"SIGNAL AGE: 69 YEARS. STILL TRANSMITTING."**

### FRAME 17 — DIRECTION
The CDTO — a composed Malaysian leader in modern professional attire, no costume — walks into the vast, quietly intelligent environment. They do not command it and make no dramatic gesture; the woven light subtly synchronises with their steady walk. Camera tracks alongside, weighted and calm. Warmth and cyan share the frame.
Caption: **"NEW NODE RECOGNIZED"**

### FRAME 18 — DAWN
FINAL HERO SHOT. The real KWSP building at dawn, aerial. Camera high and wide after a slow rise and pull-back: first warm sunlight strikes the tower, Kuala Lumpur wakes around it, traffic and life beginning. No holograms, no network graphics. Only an almost imperceptible cyan echo in one glass reflection. Natural warm daylight owns the frame. Clean title placed in the sky area: **JALINAN MERDEKA** with a small **DTD** lockup beneath.
Caption: **"TRACE COMPLETE. SIGNAL CONTINUES."**

## NEGATIVE (applies to the whole board)

No RGB chromatic-aberration glitch aesthetic, no full-colour neon wash, no decorative flare beyond the one motivated source, no soft dreamy bloom, no fisheye, no floating camera drift, no synthetic/video-game light quality, no readable fabricated text/UI/logos beyond the specified caption bars, the backwards timestamp in frame 8, the falling year fragments in frame 9, and the frame-15 origin line, no crushed/clipped blacks losing all shadow detail, no uncanny-valley facial artifacts, no malformed hands/fingers, no real named individuals, no dense tangled mass of lines at any scale in thread shots. No symmetrical/idealized facial features, no uniformly smooth skin or fabric, no evenly distributed studio-perfect lighting, no perfectly centred or mathematically balanced composition, no sterile/showroom environments, no glass-smooth camera moves without any operator weight, no digitally-perfect focus plane, no airbrushed uncanny cleanliness anywhere in frame. No generic cyberpunk, no excessive neon, no floating holograms, no glowing brains, no generic AI interfaces, no sci-fi costumes, no superhero imagery, no cheesy corporate smiles, no generic digital globes, no Malaysian flag anywhere before frame 15.
